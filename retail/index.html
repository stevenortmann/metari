Why Metari

Store automation is usually decided in the most expensive place to test it.

Vendor demos run on the vendor’s own tasks. In-store pilots pull associates off the floor, change the customer experience and take months to read. Every vendor is measured differently, so results are hard to compare.

01 · LOW COST

Test in a replica, not your stores.

No remodels, no associates pulled off shifts for weeks, no customer disruption. The work happens in Metari’s environments.

02 · SAME TASKS

Every vendor, one standard.

Each humanoid runs your tasks against your acceptance criteria, in the same room, so results can be compared side by side.

03 · EVIDENCE FIRST

Proof before a pilot.

A scorecard per task: what passed, what failed, where a person stepped in. Spend pilot money only where the evidence says it will work.

How it works

From your best associates to a deployed humanoid.

The people who already do the work well are the experts. Metari turns how they do it into data humanoid labs can train on, then measures every humanoid against it.

01

Find repeatable work

Your store task and issue records show which shelf, receiving and cleaning jobs repeat most. Those become the task order, with variations and what counts as done.

02

Learn from your associates

Experienced associates, drivers and DC workers do the work in the Metari replica, on camera. Ceiling, wall and wearable views share one clock.

03

Train

Humanoid labs train their own models on those expert demonstrations and on corrections from each attempt.

04

Validate

Every vendor runs the same tasks, including variations it has not seen. You get a scorecard per task and per vendor.

05

Dispatch and verify

Passing tasks are routed to approved humanoids in selected stores, routes or a DC. Status and evidence return to one record.

Your associates are the experts

Nothing is filmed in your stores.

Associates come to a Metari replica of your store, backroom or DC bay for scheduled sessions. They do the task the way they do it on a good day, then correct the humanoid when it gets it wrong. Those corrections are some of the most valuable data a lab can get.

  • Paid, scheduled sessions away from the sales floor
  • Consent for every recording
  • No customers, no shoppers, no patient information
  • Rights to each episode agreed with you up front
What you receive

One scorecard. Every task, every vendor.

Each attempt is scored against the acceptance criteria you wrote, across the same set of variations. Interventions are logged, and every result links back to the recorded episode.

Path to deploy

Start small. Scale what the evidence supports.

Each phase is scoped and agreed before it starts. Nothing moves into a store, route or DC until it has passed in the replica.

01

Task discovery

Rank store, backroom, delivery and DC tasks by labor hours and automation fit. No changes in your stores.

02

Replica validation

Associates demonstrate the top tasks, and humanoid vendors run them in Metari’s environments.

03

Supervised field validation

Passing tasks move into a small number of stores, routes or a DC, with people supervising and data returning.

04

Network rollout

Expand task by task and region by region. Each new site feeds the next round of training and validation.
