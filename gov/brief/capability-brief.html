Train for the task. Measure readiness.

Physical AI training data and robotics evaluation. Metari is developing instrumented environments for expert-led training data and repeatable robotics evaluation, and is seeking program and industry partners in facilities, logistics, maintenance and infrastructure operations.

Every program answers three questions first: what task the robot must perform, how it will be evaluated, and what evidence the customer receives.

Status: Campus 01 is planned, not operating. Command Center is a prototype with simulated records. All example scenarios and results are illustrative.

Proposed offerings

01

Mission-specific training data

Expert demonstrations and customer-specified sensor capture, organized around defined tasks, quality checks and delivery formats.

Robot readiness evaluation

Repeatable physical scenarios with agreed metrics, documented interventions, failure review and evidence for deployment decisions.

Dedicated program capacity

Future configured space, instrumentation and operating support under an agreed scope, subject to launch conditions.

Proposed programs. Scope, equipment, personnel, rights, pricing and availability are agreed before delivery.

Proposed deliverables

03

Program specification

Tasks, environments, variations, sensor configuration and acceptance criteria.

Training dataset

Accepted recordings, agreed annotations, calibration and sync metadata, delivery manifest.

Evaluation report

Trial conditions, outcomes, interventions, limitations and failure analysis, on held-out configurations.

Evidence index

Links each requirement to episodes, tests, reviewer decisions and artifacts.

Deliverable formats are agreed per program. No customer deliverable exists yet. Illustrative example programs are on metari.io/gov.
