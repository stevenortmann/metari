    How it works

    Two tracks, with a wall between them.

    Index already shows that people recording real work is what makes Helix generalize.F2 We would add professionals doing that work every day on one side, and rooms that never touch training on the other.

    Track 01 · Train

    Capture from people who do this all day

    • Housekeepers, laundry attendants and cooks record real hotel work in instrumented rooms.
    • Use Index, your own rigs, or both. You set the tasks and how much variety you want.
    • Shifts are scheduled, supervised and repeatable, not one-off visits.
    • Everything recorded belongs to Figure.
    No room, linen or object crosses
    Track 02 · Evaluate

    Held-out rooms, ready when Helix is

    • Rooms tagged for evaluation never appear in training capture, the same rule you used for the 30 homes.
    • Re-dressed between trials to a recipe, then checked by a supervisor before Helix goes in.
    • Every setup is logged, so a room can be repeated exactly or retired for good.
    • Your team watches the robot. Our staff handle the beds, towels and resets.
