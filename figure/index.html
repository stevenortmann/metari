The fit

Index captures the world. Metari makes the world configurable.

Figure is already building broad human-behavior data at global scale through Index. Metari should not compete with that. The complementary layer is controlled physical infrastructure: environments that can be deliberately configured, reset, repeated and measured against a specific task or failure mode.

Figure 03 is designed for home and commercial settings, while Helix targets generalization across changing physical environments. Metari turns environmental variation into an operating system: rooms, objects, routes, lighting and task conditions configured on instruction.

Natural-world scale + controlled-world depth. Broad pretraining meets repeatable environments, targeted edge cases and deployment rehearsal.
