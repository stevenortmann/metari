01The gap

Two decades of the internet made machines fluent in information. None of it taught them to make a bed, turn a hospital room, or pick an order to standard.

Physical AI has no internet to learn from. The knowledge that runs the physical economy, how expert work is performed, judged, and recovered when it goes wrong, was never written down, never structured, never made machine-readable. It lives inside the people who do the work. Metari is the infrastructure that gets it out, at scale, continuously.
