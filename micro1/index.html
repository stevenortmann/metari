    Published micro1 offering

    Data, structure and evaluation.

    • Expert demonstrations captured from first- and third-person views.
    • Data labeling, tracking, annotation, quality control and evaluation.
    • Synchronized stereo video, IMU and multicamera capture.

    Metari’s proposed contribution

    The environment and the operation.

    • Configurable hospitality and service settings, starting with a scoped pilot.
    • Industry-led task execution, scenario staging and repeatable resets.
    • Physical logistics, capture setup and agreed evidence packaging.
    • Reserved zones or future multi-site capacity when demand justifies it.
